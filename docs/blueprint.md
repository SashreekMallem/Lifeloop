# **App Name**: Life OS

## Core Features:

- Smart Assistant: AI-powered personal assistant that provides personalized recommendations and proactive assistance based on user data. Gemini is used not just for querying, but to continuously analyze live context across all user data for proactive decision-making.
- Life Dashboard: Centralized dashboard that displays key information such as tasks, meetings, health data, and personalized insights, customizable via widgets.
- Intelligent Suggestions: Smart suggestions that are driven by historical habits to offer recipes based on expiring ingredients or smart prompts that identify opportune times to connect users and their acquaintances.
- Gamified Progress: Gamified progress tracking through Life XP, awarding points and badges for completing tasks, hitting goals, social engagement, and maintaining health streaks. Data is stored in user profile with reward triggers for future integration with friend leaderboards and reward redemption.
- Curated Entertainment: Entertainment suggestions driven by historical listening and viewing behaviors, as a 'tool' for suggesting relaxing content during stressful periods.
- Context-Aware Decision Engine: Adaptive reasoning layer built with Gemini + Genkit, combining real-time behavior analysis (e.g., energy level, meeting fatigue), mood detection via health trends + interaction frequency, and historical patterns to suggest optimal timing/actions.
- User Data Integration: Integrate user data via Google Calendar, Apple Health, Notion, Spotify, Gmail, Fitbit, and Smart Home APIs using OAuth 2.0 & granular user consent. All data pulled is used to generate personal insights, not stored without permission.
- Proactive Assistant Layer: Smart Notifications Engine that learns when, how, and why to interrupt. Prioritizes what to show and what to delay with customizable frequency (e.g., silent mornings, active reminders in evenings). Integrates with iOS and Android native notification systems.
- Privacy & Data Handling: Zero PII is stored unless explicitly consented. AI operates in-device whenever possible (privacy-aware compute). Data is anonymized if used for analytics or insights.
- Morning Summary: Gemini summarizes the day’s key data from all apps for the Morning Summary.
- Mood Detection: AI analyzes sleep, step count, heart rate + calendar for Mood Detection.
- Recipe & Grocery: AI uses pantry status + habits to suggest meals for Recipe & Grocery.
- Entertainment Curator: Curates Spotify/Netflix/YT based on mood & time to provide Entertainment.
- Goal Replanning: Automatically reassigns tasks based on productivity, to assist with Goal Replanning

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep violet (#673AB7) to evoke a sense of calm, sophistication, and intelligence, reflecting the app's comprehensive life management capabilities.
- Background color: Light grey (#F0F0F0) to provide a clean and neutral backdrop, ensuring readability and focus on content.
- Accent color: Soft blue (#3F51B5) to highlight important actions and notifications, creating a clear visual hierarchy.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', sans-serif, for headlines; this font conveys a modern, precise, and fashionable feel that aligns well with a technologically-advanced lifestyle app.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for body text; offers a balanced, humanist sans-serif style that is readable and aesthetically pleasing for long blocks of text.
- Minimalist, line-based icons for clear representation of various functions and data points, enhancing usability.
- Clean and modular layout with clearly defined sections for each feature, allowing for easy navigation and customization via drag-and-drop widgets.
- Subtle animations and transitions for feedback on user interactions, creating a polished and engaging experience.
